Intro

I am mainly interested in interactive machine learning (IML) and key tools for it such as confidence bounds. I am also interested in IML problems arising from LLMs, including alignment, post-training, reasoning, and test-time scaling.

I love theory, but I am interested in how theory provides new insight and algorithms for practitioners. As such, I also work closely with Krafton AI on various aspects of LLMs and with Meta Platforms on efficient A/B testing systems.

Previously, I spent several wonderful years as an assistant professor at the University of Arizona.

I maintain most of my research/academic stuff in my lab page.
